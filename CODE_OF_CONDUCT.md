# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a respectful place to work on railway reporting tools, whatever your background, language or seniority.

**Expected**

- Be courteous and assume good faith. Disagree about the work, not the person.
- Give feedback that is specific and actionable. Receive it the same way.
- Respect that contributors write in English and Arabic and may work across time zones and working weeks.
- Keep operational and commercial information out of public threads.

**Not acceptable**

- Harassment, insults, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Publishing someone else's private information, or confidential material from any employer or client.
- Sustained disruption of discussions.

**Enforcement.** Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close discussions and block participants who breach this code. Report concerns through a private security advisory on the repository or by contacting the maintainer listed on the profile. Reports are handled in confidence.

This code follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
